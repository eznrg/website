Before you compare

Three things worth understanding

The wires stay the same

In ComEd territory, choosing a different electricity supplier does not change the local utility that delivers power to your property.

The price has several parts

Energy, transmission, and capacity may be bundled together or charged separately. The advertised rate may tell only part of the story.

The comparison changes over time

A fixed contract can look different against a utility benchmark that changes month by month. A useful comparison follows the same usage over the same period.

The headline rate is not the benchmark.

The published Price to Compare is not what a default-service customer actually pays: each month it is adjusted by the Purchased Electricity Adjustment, which can run either direction.

The two lines track each other closely for most of the record, and then separate sharply. In May 2026 the adjustment alone added 8.166¢ on top of a 9.704¢ Price to Compare, producing a realized rate of 17.870¢.
